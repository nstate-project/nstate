## First-wave priorities

1. **Fonction publique headcount** — France has one of the EU's largest public sectors (~20% of workforce)
2. **Dépenses sociales** — social spending at ~32% GDP, highest in OECD
3. **Déficit structurel** — chronic structural deficit tracked against EU Stability and Growth Pact
4. **Fraude fiscale** — DGFiP estimated tax gap (écart fiscal)

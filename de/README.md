# nstate — Germany (Deutschland)

> nstate zeigt, wie die Bundesregierung Steuergelder ausgibt — und wo die Daten das belegen.

**Status:** Seeking contributors  
**Chapter lead:** open  
**Eurostat data:** [loaded EU-wide](/eu/)

## Key data sources

| Domain | Institution | Dataset | URL |
|---|---|---|---|
| National statistics | Destatis | Statistisches Jahrbuch | https://www.destatis.de/EN/Themes/Government/Public-Finance/ |
| Tax receipts | Bundeszentralamt für Steuern | Tax revenue by type | https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Web/EN/Issues/Taxation/ |
| Public spending | Bundesministerium der Finanzen | Federal budget (Bundeshaushalt) | https://www.bundeshaushalt.de/ |
| Social welfare | BMAS | Sozialbudget | https://www.bmas.de/EN/Social-Security/ |
| Public sector pay | Destatis | Civil service wages (Öffentlicher Dienst) | https://www.destatis.de/EN/Themes/Work/Earnings/Public-Service/ |
| Open spending | Open Data Portal | Transparenzportal | https://www.govdata.de/ |

## First-wave priorities

1. **Federal civil service headcount** — Bundespersonal statistics, comparable to UK civil service data
2. **Sozialbudget (welfare spending)** — Germany's welfare state is one of the largest in the EU at ~32% GDP
3. **Bundeshaushalt vs outturn** — budget plans vs actual spend by ministry
4. **Beamte pay vs private sector** — public sector wage premium
